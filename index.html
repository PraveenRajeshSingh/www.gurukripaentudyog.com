Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Gurukripa Bricks

Standard bricks start from ₹8/piece. Premium bricks ₹10/piece. Shiv Eent Grade-A ₹12/piece. Bulk orders get special discounts. Call us for the latest pricing and deals.
We deliver across Jaunpur, Varanasi, Jalalpur, Trilochan, Kerakat and surrounding areas. Same-day delivery available within Jaunpur district for orders placed before noon.
Yes, we accept both small and bulk orders. Minimum order of 1,000 bricks. For smaller quantities, please contact us — we'll find a solution for you.
