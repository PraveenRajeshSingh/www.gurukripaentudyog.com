Our Process

Built on Quality. Fired by Passion.

Every brick passes through a rigorous 3-stage process before leaving our kiln

1
🏺
Premium Clay Selection
Only finest alluvial clay from Gomti river banks. Each batch tested for plasticity, shrinkage and mineral composition before moulding.
2
🔥
Double-Fired in Bull's Trench Kiln
Proprietary double-firing at 900–1000°C for maximum density, 10.5 N/mm² compressive strength, and complete weather resistance.
3
✅
Strict Quality Inspection
Every lot checked for uniform 230×110×70mm sizing (±3mm), colour consistency, crack absence, and water absorption <20%.
