Terms & Policies

1. Client-Side Data Privacy

Gurukripa Bricks Industry is committed to complete data privacy. Our application operates as a privacy-first Progressive Web App. All personal profile details, cart selections, and local order logs are stored locally on your device within your browser or native app storage.

2. Information Collected

When placing an order or requesting delivery tracking, you provide your Name, Mobile Number, and Delivery Site Address solely to coordinate trolley loading, driver dispatch, and WhatsApp order confirmation.

3. Zero Third-Party Monetization

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information with external marketing brokers. Your information is accessed exclusively by our kiln logistics team in Jaunpur, UP.

1. Sale & Supply Terms

All brick sales from Gurukripa Bhatta Yard are subject to physical verification at the delivery site prior to tractor/trolley unloading.

2. Transit Breakage Margin

Per Indian brick manufacturing norms (IS:1077), a standard transit margin of 3% to 5% for red clay masonry bricks is expected due to road transit on tractor trolleys.

3. Payment Terms

Orders are dispatched upon token confirmation via UPI, Bank Transfer, or verified Cash on Delivery at site. Computer-generated GST invoices reflect 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%).

4. Jurisdiction

Any disputes arising out of contracts of sale are strictly subject to Jaunpur (Uttar Pradesh) jurisdiction.

1. Dispatch Kiln Yard

All dispatches originate from Gurukripa Brick Kiln Yard-1, located on the Jaunpur – Varanasi Highway, Uttar Pradesh.

2. Delivery Fleet & Capacity

We operate our own dedicated fleet of 12 power tractor-trolleys with a standardized payload of 4,000 bricks per trolley.

3. Delivery Timelines & Radius

Same-day delivery is guaranteed for orders confirmed before 12:00 PM within Jaunpur district (including Jalalpur, Trilochan, Shahganj, and Mariahu). Deliveries to Varanasi, Azamgarh, and Sultanpur are dispatched within 24 hours.

1. IS Standard Compliance

Our Grade-A Shiv Eent and Awwal bricks are fired using a double-kiln cycle compliant with Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS:1077-1992 specifications for common burnt clay building bricks.

2. Compressive Strength & Water Absorption

Grade-A bricks achieve an average compressive strength exceeding 10.5 N/mm² with water absorption strictly below 15% by weight after 24 hours of cold immersion.

3. Sound & Ring Verification

Every batch of Grade-A bricks produces a clear metallic ringing sound when struck together, confirming uniform density and absence of internal voids.
