Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the website and the Android / iOS app

In short: the app keeps your cart, saved estimates and site photos on your own device. The only time information leaves your phone is when you tap "Order on WhatsApp" — then the order summary is handed to WhatsApp so our Jaunpur yard can dispatch your trolley.

1. Who we are

Gurukripa Bricks Industry ("we", "our") is a red clay brick manufacturer operating from the Jaunpur – Varanasi highway, Uttar Pradesh, India. You can reach us at +91 91989 23230 or info@gurukripaentudyog.com.

2. Information you give us

We only ask for what a brick delivery actually needs:

  • Name and mobile number — so the driver can reach you at the site.
  • Delivery site address and pincode — to confirm we can reach the site and which trolley route applies.
  • Road access and unloading notes — so the trolley is not sent down a lane it cannot enter.
  • Payment preference (UPI, bank transfer or cash on delivery) — recorded on the order, not processed by us.
  • Site photos — optional, only if you choose to attach one for dispatch confirmation.

3. What is stored on your device

The app is offline-first. The following is saved in your browser's / app's local storage and is not uploaded anywhere by the app itself:

DataWhereWhy
Cart, wishlist, language choice Device storage Keeps your basket between visits, including with no signal.
Saved wall estimates Device database Re-open a site calculation at a remote plot without internet.
Downloaded rate card Device database Show today's brick rates offline.
Order history and queued orders Device storage Track status and re-send an order that failed offline.
Site inspection photos Device database Attach to a dispatch request; deleted when you clear them.

You can erase all of it at any time — see section 7.

4. When information leaves your device

Only on your explicit action:

  • WhatsApp order. Tapping "Order on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (cart items, estimator dimensions, pincode, payment preference). That message is sent through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • A phone call. Tapping the call button dials our yard number.
  • Online maps. Opening the site-location link loads Google Maps, which receives the address you searched.

We do not operate an advertising network, we do not sell or rent your contact details, and we do not share them with marketing brokers. Order details are seen only by our kiln logistics team.

5. Notifications

Order-status and rate-drop alerts are off by default. They switch on only when you tap "Enable alerts", at which point your browser or operating system asks for notification permission. The permission can be revoked in system settings; the app then falls back to showing alerts inside the app only. Alert history is kept on your device.

6. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap the site-photo button. Photos are compressed and stored on your device, and are shared only as part of a WhatsApp order you send. The app does not scan, analyse or upload your photo library.

7. Your controls and deletion

  • Clear saved estimates — Estimator → Saved estimates → Clear.
  • Clear alerts — bell icon → Clear history.
  • Clear the cart — remove items in the cart drawer.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling the app, or clearing site data for this website in your browser settings, removes all locally stored data.
  • Ask us to delete an order record — call or message +91 91989 23230 with your order reference (format GBI-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) and we will remove it from our dispatch records.

8. Children

The app is a business ordering tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Security

The website and app are served over HTTPS. Because data is stored locally, the security of your saved estimates and photos also depends on your device lock. If you share a phone with others, clear your estimates after use.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way we handle data changes, this page is updated and the "Last updated" date above changes. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
